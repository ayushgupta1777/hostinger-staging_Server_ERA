NEW RAJ FANCY - STORE POLICIES

1. Return Policy:
- We offer a 7-day return policy for all unused items in their original condition and packaging.
- To initiate a return, customers must use the "Returns" section in the app.
- Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.
- Custom-stitched items or personalized items are strictly non-returnable.

2. Shipping Policy:
- Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days across India.
- Express shipping is available for select pin codes and takes 2-3 business days.
- Free shipping on all orders above ₹2000.
- Orders below ₹2000 incur a flat ₹100 shipping fee.

3. Payment Methods:
- We accept UPI, Credit/Debit cards, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD).
- COD is only available for orders up to ₹5000.

4. Quality Guarantee:
- We guarantee the authenticity of all our fabrics (silk, cotton, georgette, etc.). If you suspect a defect, please report it within 48 hours of delivery.
